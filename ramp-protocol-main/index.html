security.md

Built to move value, not custody it.

RAMP never takes custody of routed assets — it signs and forwards through the underlying bridges you'd otherwise use manually.

Non-custodial routing

Transfers move through audited third-party bridges; RAMP's contracts never hold user funds at rest.

Multisig-controlled treasury

Protocol and staking treasuries sit behind a 5-of-8 multisig with a 48-hour timelock on parameter changes.

Rate-limited routing

Per-route transfer caps and anomaly monitoring slow large or unusual flows for manual review.

Open-source contracts

Router and staking contracts are published for public review ahead of each network's mainnet route.

faq.md

Before you bridge.

is RAMP affiliated with Robinhood?+

No. RAMP (RH Asset Migration Protocol) is an independent, community-built project. "RH" refers to Robinhood Chain as a destination network — RAMP is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Robinhood Markets, Inc.

which networks can I bridge between?+

RAMP currently routes transfers between Solana, Ethereum and BNB Chain into Robinhood Chain, with return routes back out to each source chain.

what does staking $RAMP actually change?+

Staked $RAMP lowers the routing fee applied to your transfers and raises their priority when a route is congested. Fees step down across four tiers, from 0.30% unstaked to 0.05% at the Gold tier.

does RAMP hold my funds while routing?+

No. RAMP signs and forwards transfers through the underlying bridge for the selected route — it does not take custody of assets in transit.

how does the router pick a path?+

Right now every route executes through relay.link, the first network RAMP has integrated. As more bridges are added, each quote will score them live on liquidity depth, fees and confirmation time and execute the best one — still as a single transaction you approve once.
